==== BACKLOG item 24 — option (B) on the dedicated ext4 partition ====
date: 2026-09-16
cell: stub/test/aw_expert_test.c
module: srcversion 0CAA5C74C7EFBCD27125AD3 (the post-fix module)

THE SUBSTRATE IS DONE. A raw ext4 partition, nothing between the filesystem and
the device -- no LVM, no dm, no zvol -- starting at sector 100665344 (48.0 GiB).
1700 expert files of 2457600 bytes. Every one of the 1700 resolves to EXACTLY ONE
extent, flags `last,eof`, and there is not a single UNWRITTEN, DELALLOC, INLINE,
ENCODED or UNKNOWN extent in the set. That is precisely what item 24 asked for.

THE TRANSLATION IS DONE, RED-FIRST. `aw_fiemap` resolves a file to an absolute
LBA and compares the raw device against the file's bytes; dropping the partition
start (`--mutate`) makes it go red. Verified again today on the fixed module.

WHAT WAS MISSING, AND WHY NOBODY NOTICED. `aw_gguf_test` covers option (A) --
ranges inside one big shard -- and it needs an extent boundary to straddle. A
single-extent expert file has none, so that cell structurally COULD NOT cover
option (B). The layout the documentation actively RECOMMENDS was the one with no
DMA cell behind it, while the fallback was tested. `aw_expert_test` closes that.

RESULT: option (B) works. 32 experts, 78,643,200 bytes, one batch, every expert
byte-verified against the ordinary filesystem path, via_host_bounce unmoved. The
red leg drops the partition offset and fails on CONTENT, not on an error code --
which is the entire reason a content check is there: the DMA succeeds and returns
plausible bytes from 48 GiB earlier in the disk.

A DOCUMENTATION DEFECT THIS CELL CAUGHT
=======================================
`docs/USING_ARCWELL.md` §5 and `README.md` both said the layout "gives exactly
1 extent per expert and one DMA segment".

The first half is true. **The second half was false and unachievable.** A segment
is a bio; a bio holds at most BIO_MAX_VECS (256) pages = 1 MiB. These experts are
2.34 MiB, so three bios is the floor NO layout can beat. Measured: 4 bios for one
expert, settling to ~3.1/expert at scale.

  experts:      1     2     4     8    16    32
  segments:     4     8    14    26    50    99
  per expert: 4.00  4.00  3.50  3.25  3.13  3.09     floor 3.00

Linear, and converging on the floor. Nothing beyond the vector limit is splitting
these transfers.

Anyone sizing a batch against that sentence would have been wrong by a factor of
three. Both documents are corrected: what the layout actually buys is that an
expert is ONE REQUEST at one in_dest_offset, where under option (A) an expert
straddling an extent boundary becomes two. The bio count is the block layer's
business and is the same either way.

This is the fourth instance this campaign of a claim that was never measured
because the cell that would have measured it could not structurally reach it --
after item 20's fitted arithmetic, max_inflight's module-global high-water mark,
and the installer purge that ran on a host with only one version registered.

ALSO CLOSES most of item 17 (scale beyond one 1 MiB single-segment BO): these are
2.34 MiB buffers, multi-bio, 32 to a batch, byte-verified.
