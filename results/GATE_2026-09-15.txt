######## VERDICT AGAINST THE GATE'S OWN KILL CONDITIONS ########
The campaign named three ways to fail. None fired.

1. "B_P_arcwell < B_P_host_cold -> the bandwidth premise is dead."
   2.91 > 1.58. **arcwell is 1.84x the cold host path.** Not dead.
2. "B_P_host_cold already close to B_FABRIC -> no headroom."
   1.58 of 13.99 = 11%. Enormous headroom. Not dead.
3. "C_arcwell >= C_host -> the CPU component is dead."
   0.0121 vs 0.1971 = **16.3x less process CPU per GiB**; system-wide 0.0341 vs
   0.1963 = 5.8x less. Not dead.

######## THE COLD/WARM DISTINCTION DECIDED THE OUTCOME ########
Against B_P_host_warm (8.51 GB/s) arcwell loses 2.91 to 8.51 and the gate would
have closed as a verdict killing the premise. That comparison prices a DRAM hit
against a miss and is wrong; the miss tier is by definition the path taken when
the bytes are NOT in DRAM, and there both paths start at the same drive. The warm
number is retained because it bounds what residency buys (5.4x over cold), not as
a comparand.

######## WHAT THE NUMBERS SAY BEYOND PASS/FAIL ########
- arcwell at 2.91 GB/s EXCEEDS the record's ~2.3 GB/s raw NVMe figure
  (design-qwen-flash-next.md:1022). Either the drive is faster than that figure or
  the P2P path avoids overhead the earlier measurement carried. Worth one cell to
  separate; not asserted here.
- arcwell uses 21% of B_FABRIC (2.91 of 13.99). Drive-bound, not fabric-bound, as
  the fabric measurement predicted. A second drive is the lever on this axis, and
  that is a hardware decision.
- The CPU gap is the larger of the two effects and is the one that compounds:
  FreeToken routes work to the CPU when B_P is low (q* ~= m*B_P/B_H), and M14
  measured the host expert tier winning 15.0/15.5 vs 10.4/10.6 t/s when the CPU
  is free. arcwell returns ~0.185 CPU-s per GiB of miss traffic to that tier.

######## LIMITATIONS — none of these are hidden ########
1. **B_P_host is NOT arcint's staging ring.** The campaign specified
   design-qwen-flash-next.md:1315's method; this is the same SHAPE (pread into a
   pinned CL_MEM_ALLOC_HOST_PTR buffer, then clEnqueueWriteBuffer, batched) but it
   is my code, not arcint's patches/0006 path. A measurement against arcint's own
   ring may differ and is still owed.
2. **Synthetic experts.** 1700 fallocate'd files, single extent each, not the real
   UD-Q3_K_XL GGUF. Real experts live at offsets inside multi-GB shards
   (M4_API.md option A) and can span extent boundaries -> multi-segment DMA. This
   store is option B, the recommended layout, so it measures the good case.
3. **One card.** B60 only. The A770 is ineligible on bandwidth (B_FABRIC 1.84 GB/s,
   below the drive) but the CPU component should still be taken there, since that
   is the card where it dominates.
4. **2.34 GiB per run**, ~0.9-1.6 s wall. Short enough that the sampler recorded
   one sample, not a series. No memory pressure at any point.
5. Page cache was dropped before each cold leg; arcwell reads the raw device and
   never populates it, so its number is cold by construction.
