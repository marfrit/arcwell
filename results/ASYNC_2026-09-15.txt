######## A COST TO DESIGN AROUND ########
Submit is NOT free and gets worse under pressure: **9.7 ms unloaded, 42 ms each
with four batches outstanding**. It builds ~192 bios per 64-expert batch (~600
pages per expert, BIO_MAX_VECS-capped), so the cost scales with bytes queued and
with contention for the bio mempool.

Consequence for prefetch: prefer **smaller, more frequent batches** over few deep
ones. A 64-expert batch commits ~10-42 ms of submit before anything can be
collected, which is itself long enough to matter on a per-layer schedule.
