arcwell — runtime-PM protection must outlive the module (BACKLOG item 23)
GPU:  Intel Arc Pro B60 @ 0000:0f:00.0
Date: 2026-09-15

FAILURE THIS PREVENTS: runtime suspend on a GPU whose BAR is carved for p2pdma
wedges the card permanently (GT reset fails -ETIMEDOUT, xe declares it wedged,
every GEM_CREATE then returns -ECANCELED). This killed the A770 twice in one
session. The carve is devres-owned by the pci_dev and outlives the module, so a
module-lifetime pm_runtime_get_sync() is NOT sufficient: rmmod drops it and leaves
a carved GPU free to suspend.

FIRST ATTEMPT WAS WRONG, AND THE CELL CAUGHT IT. pm_runtime_forbid() was gated on
!g_carve_count -- on THIS module instance creating a carve. A later instance that
found existing carves took the aw_pages_present() short-circuit, never reached the
carve path, and never forbade PM. That is the most dangerous case of all: a carved
BAR with a module that believes it is protected.

  after carve : p2pmem=256 MiB control=auto status=active
  after rmmod : p2pmem=256 MiB control=auto status=active
  RESULT=FAIL -- carve remains but runtime PM is back to auto; the GPU can suspend and wedge

FIX: key the protection off "this BAR IS carved", not "we carved it". Every
success path in aw_ensure_carved() -- pages already present, carve already covers,
newly carved -- now calls aw_forbid_pm_once().

######## VERIFIED ########
control right after insmod (before any mapping): auto
control after a buffer was mapped             : on
forbid message count: 1
rmmod rc=0
control after rmmod: on   p2pmem still 256 MiB
RESULT=PASS -- PM protection outlived the module on a carved BAR

pm_runtime_forbid() is DEVICE state, not module state, which is the whole point:
unloading arcwell can no longer strand a carved GPU. Operators re-enable with
'echo auto > /sys/bus/pci/devices/<bdf>/power/control' only once the carves are
gone, i.e. after a rebind or reboot.
