FINDING: the B60 sits in a DIFFERENT IOMMU group from the NVMe controller and it
does not matter. dma_map_resource() maps into the NVMe device's own domain
regardless of which group the provider is in. An earlier note in this campaign
treated shared-group-0 as a reason the A770 was the right first target; that
reason does not hold. Also note distance = 6 here versus 8 for the A770, i.e. a
shorter path through the topology.
